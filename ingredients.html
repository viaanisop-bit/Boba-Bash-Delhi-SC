Taro boba

Makes one cup.

  • 3 tbsp taro powder (or mashed cooked taro root)
  • 1 cup milk (or oat milk)
  • 1/4 cup cooked tapioca pearls
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar syrup
  • A cup of ice cubes

Serve over ice with a wide straw.

Strawberry boba

Makes one cup.

  • 1/2 cup strawberries, fresh or frozen
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup cooked tapioca pearls
  • 1 tbsp sugar syrup
  • A cup of ice cubes

Serve over ice with a wide straw.

Matcha boba

Makes one cup.

  • 1 tsp matcha powder
  • 2 tbsp hot water, to whisk the matcha
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup cooked tapioca pearls
  • 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar syrup
  • A cup of ice cubes

Serve over ice with a wide straw.
